# Sample IT & Security Policy (demo data)

> This is sample content so the demo site has something to answer from.
> Replace it with your company's real IT policy.

## Laptops and Equipment

Every employee receives a company laptop on their first day. Laptops are
replaced every 3 years, or sooner if they fail. Employees may request one
external monitor, a keyboard and a mouse through the IT helpdesk.

Company equipment must be returned on your last working day. Lost or stolen
devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Passwords and Sign-in

- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.
- Passwords are changed every 90 days.
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) is required for email, chat and the HR portal.
- Never share your password, including with IT staff. IT will never ask for it.

## Wi-Fi and VPN

The office Wi-Fi network for employees is "Office-Staff". Guests use
"Office-Guest", which has no access to internal systems.

When working from home or travelling, connect to the company VPN before opening
any internal tool. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN switched on.

## Software

Only software from the company software portal may be installed on work
laptops. To request other software, raise a ticket with the IT helpdesk; most
requests are reviewed within 3 working days.

## Getting Help

The IT helpdesk is open Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 19:00. Raise a ticket in the
helpdesk portal or post in the #it-help chat channel. For urgent problems
outside these hours, such as a suspected security incident, call the on-call
IT number listed in the helpdesk portal.

## Reporting Security Incidents

Report suspicious emails, unexpected login alerts or possible data leaks to
IT immediately using the "Report phishing" button in email or the #it-help
channel. Do not forward suspicious emails to colleagues.
