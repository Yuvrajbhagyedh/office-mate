# Sample Expense & Travel Policy (demo data)

> This is sample content so the demo site has something to answer from.
> Replace it with your company's real expense policy.

## Submitting Expenses

Submit expense claims in the finance portal within 30 days of spending the
money. Attach a receipt for every item over ₹500. Claims are reviewed by your
manager and then by finance; approved claims are paid with the next monthly
salary.

## Travel Booking

All work travel must be approved by your manager before booking. Book flights
and hotels through the company travel portal so the company is billed
directly.

- Flights: economy class for all trips under 6 hours.
- Trains: AC 2-tier or equivalent.
- Hotels: up to ₹6,000 per night in metro cities and ₹4,000 per night elsewhere.

## Daily Allowance

When travelling overnight for work, employees receive a daily allowance of
₹1,500 for meals and local transport. Meals already covered by the hotel or an
event are not claimed separately.

## Local Travel

Taxis and ride-hailing for client meetings are reimbursed in full with a
receipt. Using your own car or bike for work trips is reimbursed at ₹12 per
kilometre.

## Internet and Phone

Employees who work from home receive ₹1,000 per month towards internet costs,
paid automatically with salary. Work phone bills are reimbursed up to ₹800 per
month.

## Not Reimbursed

Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and upgrades to a higher travel
class without manager approval are not reimbursed.
