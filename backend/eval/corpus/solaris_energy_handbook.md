# Solaris Energy — Employee Handbook (2024)

## Leave Policy
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Unused leave
of up to 5 days may be carried over into the following calendar year. Sick leave
is separate and capped at 10 days per year.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully
remote arrangements require Director-level sign-off and a home-office stipend of
$500 per year.

## Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses under $75 do not require a receipt. Expenses of $75 or more
must include an itemized receipt and be submitted within 30 days. Reimbursements
are paid on the 15th of each month.

## Security
All laptops must use full-disk encryption. Passwords must be at least 14
characters and rotated every 90 days. Reporting a lost device to IT Security is
mandatory within 24 hours.
